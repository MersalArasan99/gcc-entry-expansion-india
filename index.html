      That disagreement is the reason this tool exists. Each source counts something slightly different, and none shows its working. GCC Compass takes the opposite approach: every figure links to the page it came from, and anything we could not confirm against a primary page carries a Verify flag.

      How it was built

      Figures were gathered and structured with Claude (Anthropic) from public reports, state policy documents and press releases, then reviewed by hand. Salary medians and centre counts come from Flexiple's own talent data. Office rents come from CRE Matrix's Q1 2026 Grade A survey. Sector strengths per city come from JLL. State incentives come from official pages where reachable, otherwise from tax-firm summaries of the notified policy.

      Known gaps

      • Salaries are software-engineer medians. Finance, operations and R&D roles pay differently.
      • Rent is a city-wide Grade A average; micro-markets vary widely.
      • Tier-2 cities appear under Incentives only. Comparable pay and rent data for them isn't published.
      • The pipeline and provider lists are samples to prove the method, not complete registers.
